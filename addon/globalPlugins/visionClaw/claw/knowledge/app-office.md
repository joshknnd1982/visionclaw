# Word, Excel and Outlook with the keyboard

## Word, Excel and Outlook: the ribbon and the Alt key tips
Pressing Alt shows key tips, a letter for each tab of the ribbon; in Word and Excel F10 does it too. Then press
a tab's letter, and the letters of a command. Tab and Shift+Tab move through a tab's commands, the arrow keys
among them, and Space or Enter presses one. In Word and Excel, Control+F1 hides or shows the ribbon.

## Word: the ribbon tabs and their key tips
In Word, press Alt, then F for the File page, H for Home, N for Insert, G for Design, P for Layout, S for
References, M for Mailings, R for Review, or W for View. Alt, then Q goes to the search box on the ribbon, to
type what you are looking for.

## Word: moving between the document, the ribbon, the status bar and panes with F6
In Word, F6 moves between the document, a task pane, the status bar and the ribbon, and Shift+F6 goes the
other way. Control+F6 goes to the next Word window when several documents are open. Alt+F6 goes from an open
dialog back to the document, in dialogs that allow it.

## Word: saving a document, Save As, new, opening and closing
In Word, Control+S saves the document and F12 opens Save As. Control+N makes a new document and Control+O
opens one. Control+W closes the document, and Alt+F4 closes Word. Control+P prints.

## Word: bold, italic, underline and the size of the letters
In Word, Control+B makes the selected text bold, Control+I italic and Control+U underlined; Control+Shift+D
double-underlines. Control+Right bracket makes the letters one point larger and Control+Left bracket one point
smaller. Control+D opens the Font dialog.

## Word: heading styles and the Normal style
In Word, Control+Alt+1 gives the paragraph the Heading 1 style, Control+Alt+2 Heading 2, and Control+Alt+3
Heading 3. Control+Shift+N puts it back to the Normal style. Control+Shift+S opens the Apply Styles pane.
Control+Shift+L makes a bulleted list.

## Word: centering and aligning paragraphs, and line spacing
In Word, Control+E centers the paragraph, Control+L aligns it left, Control+R right, and Control+J justifies
it. Control+1 gives single line spacing, Control+2 double and Control+5 one and a half. Control+M indents the
paragraph, Control+Shift+M takes the indent away, and Control+Q removes paragraph formatting.

## Word: going to a page, finding text, and the last change
In Word, Control+G or F5 opens Go To, to go to a page, bookmark, footnote, table, comment or picture.
Control+F opens the Navigation pane to search the document, and Control+H finds and replaces. Shift+F5
goes back to the last place that was changed.

## Word: moving by word and paragraph, and selecting a paragraph
In Word, Control+Left and Right arrow move by word, and Control+Up and Down arrow by paragraph. Control+Shift+Down
arrow selects to the end of the paragraph and Control+Shift+Up arrow to its start. F8 pressed again and again
selects the word, sentence, paragraph, section and document; Shift+F8 makes it smaller.

## Word: checking spelling and grammar with F7
In Word, F7 opens the Editor pane to check the spelling and grammar of the document or of the selected text.
Alt+F7 goes to the next spelling or grammar mistake. Shift+F7 opens the Thesaurus.

## Word: comments, track changes and page breaks
In Word, Control+Alt+M inserts a comment. Control+Shift+E turns track changes on or off. Control+Enter inserts
a page break. Control+Z undoes and Control+Y redoes.

## Excel: the ribbon tabs and their key tips
In Excel, press Alt, then F for the File menu, H for Home, N for Insert, P for Page Layout, M for Formulas, A
for Data, R for Review, or W for View. Alt, then Q goes to the search box on the ribbon.

## Excel: moving between the sheet, the ribbon and panes with F6
In Excel, F6 moves between the worksheet, the ribbon, a task pane and the zoom controls, and Shift+F6 goes the
other way. Control+F6 switches between two Excel windows.

## Excel: moving from cell to cell
In Excel, the arrow keys move one cell, Tab one cell to the right and Shift+Tab one to the left. Enter finishes
an entry and moves down, and Shift+Enter moves up. Control+arrow key goes to the edge of the data. Home goes to
the start of the row, Control+Home to the start of the sheet, and Control+End to the last used cell.

## Excel: moving between sheets of a workbook
In Excel, Control+Page Down goes to the next sheet in the workbook and Control+Page Up to the sheet before.
Shift+F11 inserts a new sheet. Control+Shift+Page Down selects this sheet and the next one.

## Excel: going to a cell with F5
In Excel, F5 opens the Go To dialog; type the address of the cell and press Enter.

## Excel: editing a cell with F2
In Excel, F2 edits the active cell with the cursor at the end of what is in it. Enter finishes and Escape
cancels. Alt+Enter starts a new line in the same cell. Delete clears what is in the selected cells. Shift+F2
adds or edits a note, and Control+Shift+F2 a threaded comment.

## Excel: selecting a row, a column or a range of cells
In Excel, Shift+Space selects the whole row and Control+Space the whole column. Shift+arrow keys make the
selection one cell larger, and Control+Shift+arrow keys extend it to the last cell with something in it.
Control+A selects the current region, and pressing it again and again the whole sheet.

## Excel: saving, spelling, sums, the date and formatting cells
In Excel, Control+S saves and F12 opens Save As. F7 checks spelling. Alt+Equal sign inserts a Sum formula.
Control+Semicolon enters today's date and Control+Shift+Colon the time. Control+1 opens Format Cells, and
Control+B, Control+I and Control+U make bold, italic and underlined.

## Outlook: new message, reply, reply all, forward and send
In Outlook, Control+N creates a new message in Mail, and in classic Outlook Control+Shift+M does it from
anywhere. Control+R replies, Control+Shift+R replies to all, and Control+F forwards. Control+Enter sends the
message; in classic Outlook Alt+S sends it too.

## Outlook: going to Mail, Calendar and People
In Outlook, Control+1 goes to Mail, Control+2 to the Calendar, and Control+4 to People. In classic Outlook,
Control+Shift+I goes to the Inbox and Control+Shift+O to the Outbox.

## Outlook: moving between folders and the message list
In Outlook, Control+Y goes to the folders. There the arrow keys move, the first letter of a name jumps to that
folder, and Right and Left arrow open and close a folder's subfolders. In classic Outlook, Space or Enter goes
to the message list, and F6 or Tab moves between the main window, the folders, the Reading pane and To-Do bar.

## Outlook: reading the message list
In Outlook's message list, Delete deletes a message, Control+Q marks it read, Control+U unread, and Insert
flags it. In classic Outlook, Up and Down arrow move through the messages and Enter opens one. In the new
Outlook, Control+> goes to the next message, Control+< to the one before, and Shift+Enter opens one.

## Outlook: searching mail and checking for new mail
In classic Outlook, Control+E or F3 goes to the search box, Escape clears the search, and Control+M or F9
checks for new messages. In the new Outlook, Escape cancels a search.

## Outlook: the new Outlook and classic Outlook
Microsoft lists keys separately for the new Outlook and classic Outlook, and some differ. In the new Outlook,
pressing the question mark key shows its list of keyboard shortcuts.
