# Chrome, Edge and Firefox with the keyboard

## Chrome, Edge and Firefox: going to the address bar
Control+L or Alt+D moves to the address bar in Chrome, Edge and Firefox. F6 also does it in Chrome and
Firefox, and F4 in Edge; in Firefox press F6 only once. Type an address, or words to search for, and press
Enter.

## Chrome, Edge and Firefox: searching the web from the address bar
Control+K or Control+E moves to the address bar, ready for words to search the web, in Chrome, Edge and
Firefox. In Firefox, when its separate search bar is shown, the same keys go to the search bar instead.

## Chrome and Firefox: typing a site name and adding www and .com
In the address bar, type the name of a site and press Control+Enter. Chrome adds www. and .com around it and
opens it; Firefox completes it with .com.

## Chrome, Edge and Firefox: opening a new tab or a new window
Control+T opens a new tab and Control+N opens a new window, in Chrome, Edge and Firefox.

## Chrome, Edge and Firefox: a private window, called Incognito or InPrivate
Chrome calls it an Incognito window and opens it with Control+Shift+N. Edge calls it an InPrivate window and
also opens it with Control+Shift+N. Firefox calls it a private window and opens it with Control+Shift+P.

## Chrome, Edge and Firefox: switching between tabs
Control+Tab or Control+Page Down goes to the next tab, and Control+Shift+Tab or Control+Page Up to the tab
before. Control+1 to Control+8 go to that tab, and Control+9 to the last tab. In Firefox, Control+Tab can be
set in Settings to go through the tabs in the order they were last used.

## Chrome, Edge and Firefox: closing a tab or the whole window
Control+W or Control+F4 closes the tab in use; in Firefox they do not close a pinned tab. Control+Shift+W or
Alt+F4 closes the whole window with all its tabs, in Chrome, Edge and Firefox.

## Chrome, Edge and Firefox: reopening a tab that was closed by mistake
Control+Shift+T reopens the tab that was closed last, in Chrome, Edge and Firefox. In Firefox, pressing it again
reopens the tabs or windows before it, in the order they were closed, and Control+Shift+N reopens the last
closed window.

## Chrome, Edge and Firefox: finding text on the page
Control+F opens the find bar in Chrome, Edge and Firefox; type the words and press Enter. F3 also opens it in
Chrome and Edge. In Chrome and Firefox, Control+G goes to the next match and Control+Shift+G to the one before.
In Firefox, F3 and Shift+F3 do the same, and Escape closes the find bar.

## Firefox: quick find with the slash and the apostrophe
In Firefox, the slash key starts a quick find on the page, and the apostrophe starts a quick find that looks only
in the text of links. Escape closes it.

## Chrome, Edge and Firefox: going back and forward, reloading and stopping
Alt+Left arrow goes back and Alt+Right arrow forward. F5 or Control+R reloads the page. Control+Shift+R reloads
it without the stored copy, as does Shift+F5 in Chrome and Edge and Control+F5 in Firefox. In Chrome and
Firefox, Escape stops a page loading and Alt+Home opens the home page.

## Chrome, Edge and Firefox: the list of downloads
Control+J opens the downloads in Chrome, Edge and Firefox.

## Chrome, Edge and Firefox: history and clearing browsing data
Control+H opens the history in Chrome and Edge, and the history sidebar in Firefox; Control+Shift+H opens
Firefox's Library window with the history. Control+Shift+Delete opens the choices for clearing browsing data,
called Clear Recent History in Firefox.

## Chrome, Edge and Firefox: bookmarks, called favorites in Edge
Control+D bookmarks the page, or adds it to the favorites in Edge. Control+Shift+B shows or hides the bookmarks
bar, called the favorites bar in Edge. Control+Shift+O opens the Bookmark Manager in Chrome, the favorites in
Edge, and the Library with all bookmarks in Firefox. Control+B opens Firefox's bookmarks sidebar.

## Chrome, Edge and Firefox: zooming in and out, and full screen
Control+Plus zooms in, Control+Minus zooms out, and Control+0 goes back to the normal size, in Chrome, Edge
and Firefox. F11 turns full screen on and off.

## Chrome: moving between the toolbars, dialogs and the page with F6
In Chrome, F6 moves through the toolbars and the address bar, and to a dialog that is open but not in focus.
Control+F6 skips back to the web page. Alt+Shift+A also moves to a dialog that is open but not in focus.

## Edge: moving between panes and the page with F6
In Edge, F6 moves to the next pane of the window and Shift+F6 to the one before. Control+F6 goes to the web
page itself.

## Firefox: F6 for the address bar, frames and pop-ups
In Firefox, F6 moves to the address bar. On the page, F6 moves to the next frame or pop-up and Shift+F6 to the
one before. Tab and Shift+Tab move between the links and fields of the page.

## Chrome and Edge: the toolbar and the browser's main menu
Alt+Shift+T moves to the first item on the toolbar in Chrome and Edge. In Chrome, F10 moves to the last item
of the toolbar. Alt+F or Alt+E opens Chrome's menu and Edge's Settings and more menu; in Edge, F10 and then
Enter opens it too.

## Firefox: the menu bar and the context menu
In Firefox, Alt or F10 shows the menu bar for a moment so that its menus can be used, and Escape closes a menu.
Shift+F10 opens the context menu.

## Chrome, Edge and Firefox: caret browsing with F7
F7 turns caret browsing on or off in Chrome, Edge and Firefox.

## Edge and Firefox: Immersive Reader, Read Aloud and Reader View
In Edge, F9 opens Immersive Reader and Control+Shift+U turns Read Aloud on or off. In Firefox, F9 or
Control+Alt+R turns Reader View on or off.

## Firefox: muting a tab and moving a tab
In Firefox, Control+M mutes or unmutes the sound of the tab. Control+Shift+Page Up moves the tab one place to
the left and Control+Shift+Page Down one place to the right.
