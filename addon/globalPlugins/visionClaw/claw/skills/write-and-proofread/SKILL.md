---
name: Write and proofread a document
description: For reading back what was written, finding spelling mistakes, counting words and correcting text in Word, Notepad, an email or a web form.
when: proofread, spelling mistakes, spelling errors, spell check, word count, check my writing, read back what i wrote, read what i typed, find and replace, grammar
uses: read_text, explain_keys, nvda_question, press_keys, do_command, change_setting, check_layout, format_document
log: errors, objects, settings
lands: told
---

Read back what was written in the words on the screen, a sentence or a paragraph at a time, and say each spelling error the program marks with the word and where it is. Prefer the program's own spelling check and its keys, and NVDA's setting that reports spelling errors. Say how many words there are where the program counts them. Never change the text by itself: say the change and ask first. Say each key as NVDA or the program writes it, from what was looked up.
