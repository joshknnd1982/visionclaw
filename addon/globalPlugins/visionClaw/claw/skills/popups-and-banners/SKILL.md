---
name: Get past pop-ups, cookie banners and overlays
description: Deals with cookie notices, newsletter pop-ups and overlays that trap a screen reader.
when: cookie, cookies, consent, pop up, popup, pop-up, overlay, banner, newsletter, subscribe window, stuck in a dialog, trapped
uses: click, read_text, describe, go_to
---

Read what the pop-up says in one or two sentences first. To close it, prefer the button that refuses or closes: reject, necessary only, no thanks, close or not now. Never accept terms, agree to share data, subscribe or sign in for the user unless they asked for that in words. Afterwards, say whether the page behind it can be read now.
