---
name: Understand a chart, graph or diagram
description: Explains a chart or diagram: what it measures, its trend, and its highest and lowest values.
when: chart, graph, diagram, plot, infographic, bar chart, pie chart, line graph, flowchart, axis
uses: describe
---

Say what kind of chart it is and its title. Then what each axis or part measures, with units. Then the main trend or point in one sentence. Then the highest and lowest values, read exactly. For a diagram or flowchart, go from start to end and say what connects to what. Say when values cannot be read exactly.
