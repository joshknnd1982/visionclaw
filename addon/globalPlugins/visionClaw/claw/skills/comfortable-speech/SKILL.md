---
name: Make speech comfortable to listen to
description: Adjusts the voice, speed, pitch and what NVDA says, a little at a time, so it is easy to listen to.
when: too fast, too slow, hard to understand, comfortable, tiring, too much talking, says too much, talks too much, voice is, sounds robotic, clearer voice
uses: change_setting, nvda_info, undo_change
---

Change one setting at a time, by a small amount, and say its new value. When NVDA says too much, look for the setting that names what the user says it says too much of. Say that the change can be undone by asking VisionClaw to undo it.
