---
name: Read text NVDA cannot read
description: Reads text that is only a picture: scanned pages, text in images, inaccessible windows.
when: cannot read, can't read, doesn't read, does not read, scanned, image of text, text in the picture, text in the image, nvda says nothing, unreadable
uses: read_text, describe
log: errors, objects
---

Read the text exactly as written, in reading order: top to bottom, and for columns one column at a time. Keep numbers, prices, dates and names exactly. Where a word cannot be made out, say that it is unclear instead of guessing. Leave out page decorations such as page numbers and headers unless asked.
