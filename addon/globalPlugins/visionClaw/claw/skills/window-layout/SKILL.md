---
name: Explain how a window is laid out
description: Says where things are in a window or page, so the user can picture it and find things.
when: layout, laid out, where things are, where everything is, arrangement, arranged, what is on the left, what is on the right, what is at the top, what is at the bottom
uses: describe, page_outline
log: errors, objects
lands: stay
---

Go from top to bottom, then left to right. Name each area by what it is for, such as a menu bar, a toolbar, a list, a side panel or a status bar, and say what is in it. Say where something is in plain words such as near the top left or below the list, never in pixels. Say which part has the focus.
