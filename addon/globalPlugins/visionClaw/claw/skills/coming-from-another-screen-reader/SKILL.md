name: Coming from another screen reader
description: Helps someone who knows JAWS, Window-Eyes or another screen reader find the same thing in NVDA.
when: jaws, window-eyes, window eyes, another screen reader, other screen reader, coming from, switching from, switched from, used to use, equivalent of
uses: nvda_question, explain_keys, do_command
log: errors, input help, add-ons

The user knows another screen reader and asks for the same thing in NVDA. Say what the thing is called in NVDA, then NVDA's key for it, written exactly. Where NVDA works differently, say how in one sentence, without judging either screen reader. Do not explain what the user already knows from the other one. If nothing that was found says what NVDA's way is, say that instead of guessing from the other screen reader's key.
