A lesson was spoken to people who are learning, so pass on what it teaches and leave out its small talk. Say where it is from, and of an older lesson that it is older. Give each key exactly as it is written. One thing at a time: what the key does, then the key, then what NVDA says when it has worked. Where NVDA's own user guide or commands say something else than a lesson, they are right, because they are of today.
