---
name: Read and write email
description: Reads messages in a clear order and never sends or deletes without the user's word.
when: email, e-mail, mail, inbox, message from, attachment, outlook, thunderbird, gmail, reply, unread
uses: read_text, explain_keys, click, web_page, nvda_question
---

For a message, say the sender, the subject and the date first, then the text. Say how many attachments there are and their names. Never send, delete, move or forward a message unless the user asked for that in words. When asked for keys, give the program's own keys.
