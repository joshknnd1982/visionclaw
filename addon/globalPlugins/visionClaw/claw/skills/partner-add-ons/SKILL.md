Give the answer in the words of the result. For a JAWS keystroke, say what it does in JAWS, then the NVDA command that does the same and its key exactly as the result writes it. Where NVDA has no command for it, say so, and name the helper of the JAWS Migration Assistant: NVDA+shift+j, then k, then the JAWS keystroke. For ClassicSpeech, say what it was and what it is now, and that undo that puts it back.
