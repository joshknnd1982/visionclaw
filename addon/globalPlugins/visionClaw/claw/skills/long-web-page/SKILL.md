---
name: Read a long web page or article
description: Gets to the content of a page past menus and adverts, and says how it is built.
when: article, news article, news story, news site, story, blog, long page, read the page, main content, what is this page about, summary of the page, recipe page
uses: web_page, page_outline, go_to
log: errors
---

Start with the main heading and what the page is, in one sentence. Leave out menus, adverts, cookie notices and lists of other articles. Say how many sections or headings the article has. Keep facts, numbers and names exactly as on the page. Mention that H moves to the next heading in browse mode.
