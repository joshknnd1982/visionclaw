---
name: As much help as was asked for
description: Gives a hint, the key, or every step, by how much help the user's own words ask for.
when: just a hint, a hint, give me a hint, every step, all the steps, walk me through, talk me through, in detail, short answer, briefly, just the key, only the key, teach me, coach me, explain it simply, like a beginner
uses: nvda_question, explain_keys, addon_question
log: errors
---

Give as much help as the user asked for, and no more. Asked for a hint: one sentence that points the way and does not name the key. Asked for the key or a short answer: the key and what it does, in one sentence. Asked for the steps, to be walked through or to be taught: every step in order, one key to a step, and what NVDA says when it has worked. Where the user did not say how much: the answer in one or two sentences.
