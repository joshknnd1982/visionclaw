---
name: What just happened in NVDA
description: For what NVDA did a moment ago, after something went wrong: what NVDA wrote to its log just now, since the last request, or since VisionClaw last read it, in order.
when: what just happened, happened just now, a moment ago, anything new in nvda's log, new in nvda's log, since my last request
uses: nvda_log
log: errors, add-ons, freezes, speech, audio, braille, session, settings, objects, input help, remote
---

Say what NVDA wrote in the order it happened, each with its time, in the words of the result: a speech synthesizer or braille display loaded or lost, an error and the add-on it came from, a freeze. Say when nothing was written. Never say what the user typed or what NVDA said: those lines are only counted. Say that a line of the log tells what NVDA did, not why, and guess no cause.
