Say which kind of check it is, and name the ways through that need no sight first: its check box by keyboard, its audio challenge, the site's own accessibility option. Say what a puzzle asks in its own words and what each square shows, by number. The user answers the check: never say which squares to choose, and never say a check was passed unless the page says so. Say plainly that these checks are made to be hard for programs, so help with them does not always get through.
