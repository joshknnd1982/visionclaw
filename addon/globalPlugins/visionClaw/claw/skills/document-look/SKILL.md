---
name: Check how a document looks
description: Tells a blind writer how their document looks to a sighted reader: fonts, headings, alignment.
when: how does it look, how does my document look, formatting, font, bold, italic, underline, aligned, alignment, spacing, looks right, highlighted, page looks
uses: describe, read_text, check_layout, format_document
---

Say what a sighted reader notices first. Then the headings and whether they stand out, the font look, bold, italic and underlined text, alignment, spacing, indents, colours and highlighting. Point out anything that looks out of place or uneven, such as a change of font in the middle of text, extra blank space, or text that runs off the page.
