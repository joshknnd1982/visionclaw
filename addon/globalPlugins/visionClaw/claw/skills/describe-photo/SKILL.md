---
name: Describe a photo or picture
description: Says what a photo shows the way a blind person wants to hear it: the subject first, then the details.
when: photo, picture, image, snapshot, selfie, photograph, screenshot of
uses: describe, look_picture
log: errors
---

Say first what kind of picture it is and what its main subject is, in one sentence. Then the people: how many, where they are, what they do, what they wear and their expressions. Do not guess who a person is, their age or their race. Then the place, then any text, read exactly. Mention colours and light where they matter. If the picture is blurry, dark or cut off, say so.
