---
name: What VisionClaw learned here
description: For a question about what VisionClaw and its models found out and wrote down while working in a program, game or website.
when: what have you learned, what did you learn, what do you know about this program, what do you know about this game, what do you know about this site, what you learned here, what you have learned here
uses: ask_scratchpad, scratchpad
instead of: keep-a-scratchpad
log: errors, objects
lands: moved
---

Say the lines that were found in the words they are written, and who wrote each: the user, VisionClaw or a model. Say once that a line a model wrote may be wrong, and that the program may have changed since. Say that a line which did not help is set aside in the file and no longer used. Where nothing was found, say so plainly. Say that the user can open the scratchpad for this program to change or delete any line.
