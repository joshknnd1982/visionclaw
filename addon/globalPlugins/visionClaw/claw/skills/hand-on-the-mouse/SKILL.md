The user has a hand on the mouse here and cannot see the pointer. That is their choice, so help with it and do not send them back to the keyboard. NVDA says what is under the pointer as it moves when mouse tracking is on; NVDA+M turns it on and off. NVDA's mouse settings, which NVDA+control+M opens, can also play beeps that tell where the pointer is. Say where to move as a direction and roughly how far from where the pointer is, never in pixels. Say what is under the pointer before they click.
