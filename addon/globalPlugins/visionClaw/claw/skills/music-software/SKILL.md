Prefer taking hold of a knob or fader so that the arrow keys work it, and say its value as shown. Name controls by their printed label. Many plug-ins draw their own controls, so describe what the picture shows when NVDA finds nothing. For REAPER, the OSARA extension makes it speak with NVDA.
