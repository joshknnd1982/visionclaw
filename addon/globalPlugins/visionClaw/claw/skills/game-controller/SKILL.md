Controller mode is turned on with NVDA+alt+j, or by holding the controller's View and Menu buttons together for a second. The left stick moves the mouse pointer, slowly or fast by how far it is pushed. A clicks, B right-clicks, X double-clicks, Y says what is under the pointer. The right trigger is the mouse button for as long as it is pulled; the left trigger presses like a pen, as hard as it is pulled. The Menu button says all of it. Call buttons by the names on the controller.
