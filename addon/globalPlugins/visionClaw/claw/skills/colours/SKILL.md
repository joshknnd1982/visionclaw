Name colours with common words and say whether they are light or dark, bright or muted. Name patterns such as stripes, checks or flowers. When asked whether things go together, say what most people would think and that it is a matter of taste. Say when the light makes colours hard to tell. Where a colour was measured from the picture, say the measured colour first.
