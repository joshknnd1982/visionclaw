Lines from NVDA's developer guides may come with the request. Write the names of NVDA's functions and modules exactly as those lines and the notes write them, and never make up a name. Take from the lines only what this add-on needs, and leave a line about something else alone. The add-on still does only what was asked, speaks in short plain words, and is worked from the keyboard.
