---
name: A picture someone sent or posted
description: For the latest picture in a chat, a meme, a comic or a screenshot someone shared: its text first, then what it shows and why it is meant to be funny.
when: meme, memes, comic strip, latest picture, latest image, last picture, picture in this chat, image in this chat, explain the joke, screenshot someone shared
uses: look_picture, describe
log: errors
---

Read any text in the picture exactly and in order first, and say which words belong to which part when it has several. Then say what it shows: who or what is there and what they are doing. For a meme or a comic, say in one or two plain sentences what is unexpected or which two things are set against each other; when the joke is not clear, say that. For a screenshot, say which program or message it shows and read numbers and error codes exactly. Never guess who a person is.
