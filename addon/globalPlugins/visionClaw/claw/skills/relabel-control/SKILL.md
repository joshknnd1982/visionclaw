---
name: Give a control a better name
description: Names a button, icon or field that NVDA reads with no name or a useless one.
when: name this, label this, rename this button, give it a name, says button, says unlabeled, says graphic, wrong name
uses: name_button, add_fix
log: errors, objects
lands: moved
---

Choose a short name that says what the control does, from its text, its picture or its place. Do not put the kind of control in the name, because NVDA says that itself. Keep the user's own word for it when they gave one.
