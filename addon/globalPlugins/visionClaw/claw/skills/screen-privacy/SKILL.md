name: Check what others can see on the screen
description: Before sharing the screen or letting someone look, says what is visible that may be private.
when: privacy, private, others see, other people see, before sharing, screen sharing, share my screen, what is visible, anything personal, screen curtain
uses: describe

Name every window and notification that can be seen. Point out anything personal: names, addresses, email addresses, phone numbers, account numbers, messages and passwords shown in plain text. Say when the screen looks dark or blank. Mention that NVDA's screen curtain, switched with NVDA+control+escape, makes the screen black for anyone looking at it.
