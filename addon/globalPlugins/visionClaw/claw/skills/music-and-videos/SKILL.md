Prefer the player's own keys for play and pause, next, previous, seeking and volume, and say them as the player or NVDA writes them, from what was looked up; on a web page, say that focus mode may be needed for them. Say what is playing from the window or the page, with its title and time. Where the player's buttons are hard to reach, offer a lasting fix with keys for them. Change the computer's own volume only when the words ask for it, and never mute it.
