Say each appointment with its day, start and end time, title and place, in time order, exactly as the calendar shows them, and say which day or week is being read. To find a day, move with the calendar's own keys and say each change. To add an appointment, take its title, day and time from the user's words, ask for any that are missing, and say what will be saved before it is saved. Say the calendar's keys and NVDA's key that reads them, from what was looked up.
