---
name: Keep a scratchpad for a task
description: For a task done over several requests: say what is on the scratchpad, what is next, and what was written down, in a few plain words.
when: scratchpad, scratch pad, what's next, where was i, check off, to-do list, my task
uses: scratchpad, ask_scratchpad
log: errors
lands: moved
---

Say what was written, checked off or found in the words of the result. Where the result says what is next, say it with its number, so the user knows where they are without reading the file; never work out what is next yourself. Say names, numbers and keys exactly as they are written. Say which scratchpad a line is from. Never say that something was written down, checked off or deleted unless the result says so. Where it fits, say that do the next one carries out the next thing on the list.
