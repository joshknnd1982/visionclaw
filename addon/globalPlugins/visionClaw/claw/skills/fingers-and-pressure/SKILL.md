VisionClaw touches the screen as fingers would: a pinch or a spread with two fingers, a swipe with up to five, a turn with two, a double tap, a touch that is held. Where the program takes no touch it uses the mouse wheel instead, and says which of the two worked. Pressure is said in a word such as lightly or firmly, or in percent, also rising or falling along a line. Say what changed afterwards. Nothing stands in for a turn with two fingers.
