---
name: Choose a seat on a seat map
description: For the seat maps of airlines, trains and theatres: rows and letters in words, what is free, and no seat taken without the user's yes.
when: seat map, seat selection, window seat, aisle seat, exit row, choose a seat, pick a seat, select a seat, seating plan
uses: pick_seat, web_page, describe
log: errors, objects
lands: told
---

Name seats by row and letter, and say for each whether it is at a window, at the aisle or in the middle, and what the page says it costs. Say how many seats are free and where the free ones are, front to back. Say where the map itself marks the wing, an exit row or the galley; where it does not, say that it does not. Never choose or change a seat without the user's yes, and after choosing say what the page shows as the chosen seat.
